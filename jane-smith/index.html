Session 2 — Applied AI for Operations & Marketing

AI Inbox & Productivity System

Pain Point
Spending 2+ hours every day managing emails and writing meeting follow-ups, leaving almost no time for strategic leadership work.
Solution
Built an inbox triage system using Claude that categorizes every email into Respond, Delegate, or Archive in seconds, and generates a decision brief before every key meeting.
AI in Action
Full inbox processed in under 20 minutes. Meeting briefs generated from voice notes. A week of LinkedIn content produced from a single idea.

Session 3 — Agentic AI

Board-Prep Briefing Agent

Pain Point
Preparing for board meetings and leadership presentations was taking half a day — pulling together context, options, and recommendations from multiple sources.
Solution
Built a Board-Prep Briefing Agent on Claude with a custom system prompt that takes raw context and produces a structured one-page brief with background, options, recommended path, and next steps.
AI in Action
Full board briefing produced in under 5 minutes from a short voice note or bullet points — ready to send to stakeholders before any meeting.
Claude Claude Projects

Session 4 — Embedding AI into Workflow & Leadership Strategy

AI Sales Proposal Generator

Pain Point
After every client discovery call, writing a formal proposal from scratch was taking 3–4 hours and quality varied depending on who wrote it.
Solution
Built a Sales Proposal Generator app in Base44 that reads uploaded meeting notes and auto-drafts a complete, formatted client proposal in seconds. Also used NotebookLM to create a mini-podcast summary of a future of work report for the team.
AI in Action
Team can now generate a polished client proposal in under 60 seconds from raw discovery call notes — consistent, professional, and ready to send.
Claude Base44 NotebookLM
